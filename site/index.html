TRUST YOUR FRIENDS. CHECK THE ITEMS.

A little accountability.
A better server.

For the friend who always “found” another stack of diamonds. Keep the fun, keep the evidence.

▦
01 / TRACK

Follow the items

Record Creative item additions and privileged grants. Item provenance helps you identify where those suspicious stacks came from.

≡
02 / INVESTIGATE

Pull up the receipts

Search the audit trail by player, item, or session. Durable server logs give you evidence long after the argument in chat.

♜
03 / TAKE CONTROL

Your server. Your rules.

Configure staff alerts and review suspicious items. Preview and confirm quarantine of matching items when you need to take action.

An audit tool for Creative-item abuse. It records evidence; it does not automatically prevent every kind of cheating.

QUICK SETUP

Less setup.
More block game.

Install on your Forge server. Players don’t need the mod for server auditing; the optional client adds item badges and tooltips.

Read the full setup guide ↗
  1. 01

    Grab the JAR

    Download CreativeGuard for Minecraft 1.20.1.

  2. 02

    Drop it into mods/

    Use Forge 47.4.0 or later 47.x and Java 17. Restart your server.

  3. 03

    Check in as an operator

    Run /creativeaudit status to check setup.
    Use /creativeaudit player Steve to investigate.
